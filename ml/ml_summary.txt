Baseline ML summary for FoF-derived fragment statistics only.

This workflow predicts FoF extraction outputs from simulation-level metadata.
It does not model moon formation, bound debris, orbital capture, or other orbital outcomes.

Datasets evaluated: full, clean_subset
Targets evaluated: fragment_count_min_particles, largest_fragment_particle_count, largest_fragment_mass_kg

Dataset definitions:
- full: All simulations in outputs/fof_outcomes.csv. (489 rows)
- clean_subset: Subset with timestep == 90000, resolution_code == n65, fof_linking_length == 0.004, excluding special_case_code. (166 rows)
